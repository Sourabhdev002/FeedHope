# KPI Dashboard

This document defines the Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) that FeedHope will monitor using the integrated analytics platform to evaluate business health and product engagement.

## Core KPIs

### 1. Acquisition & Activation
- **Registration Conversion Rate**
  - **Definition:** Percentage of users who visit the landing page and successfully trigger `user_registered`.
  - **Target:** > 15%
  - **Why it matters:** Evaluates the effectiveness of the marketing copy and the friction of the signup form.

- **Onboarding Completion Rate**
  - **Definition:** Percentage of users who trigger `onboarding_started` that successfully trigger `onboarding_completed`.
  - **Target:** > 80%
  - **Why it matters:** A long or confusing onboarding wizard will lose users before they even experience the core product value.

### 2. Engagement & Retention
- **Day 1 Retention**
  - **Definition:** Percentage of users who return and trigger `dashboard_viewed` exactly 1 day after `user_registered`.
  - **Target:** > 40%
  - **Why it matters:** Evaluates if the initial Health Plan generation was compelling enough to bring them back the next day.

- **Day 7 Retention**
  - **Definition:** Percentage of users who return on Day 7.
  - **Target:** > 20%
  - **Why it matters:** Indicates early habit formation and sustained product value.

- **Daily Active Users (DAU) & Weekly Active Users (WAU)**
  - **Definition:** Count of unique users triggering `dashboard_viewed` within a 24-hour and 7-day rolling window, respectively.
  - **Target:** Month-over-Month growth.
  - **Why it matters:** The standard metric for absolute platform growth and usage scale.

### 3. Feature Usage & Product Health
- **Daily Check-in Completion Rate**
  - **Definition:** Percentage of DAUs who trigger `daily_checkin_completed` (hitting 100% of their daily targets).
  - **Target:** > 30%
  - **Why it matters:** Measures if the goals generated by the AI are achievable and if the tracking UI is motivating enough to complete.

- **Average Session Duration**
  - **Definition:** Time elapsed between the first event and the last event of a user's session.
  - **Target:** 1 - 3 minutes
  - **Why it matters:** FeedHope is designed to be frictionless. Very long sessions might indicate UX confusion, while very short sessions (< 10s) might indicate bounce or lack of engagement.
