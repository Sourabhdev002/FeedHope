# FeedHope – Personal Health Platform: Product Requirements Document

## Problem Statement
Despite the abundance of health and fitness apps, users struggle to form lasting, healthy habits. Generic advice fails to account for individual lifestyles, psychological barriers, and changing daily contexts. There is a need for a deeply personalized system that adapts to the user's progress and setbacks in real-time.

## Target Audience
- **Primary:** Health-conscious professionals (ages 25-45) looking to optimize their daily routines, manage stress, and improve fitness amidst busy schedules.
- **Secondary:** Individuals managing chronic lifestyle conditions who need structured, incremental habit changes to improve their quality of life.

## User Personas
1. **Busy Professional (Sarah, 32):** Wants to eat better and exercise regularly but travels often and works long hours. Needs micro-habits and high flexibility.
2. **Data-Driven Optimizer (Alex, 28):** Wears a smartwatch, tracks sleep, and wants a system to connect the dots between their habits and outcomes.
3. **Overwhelmed Beginner (David, 45):** Knows he needs to get healthier but has failed past diets. Needs high empathy, low-friction entry, and constant positive reinforcement.

## User Journey
1. **Onboarding & Authentication:** User downloads the app, securely creates an account, and sets up their profile.
2. **Health Assessment:** User completes a comprehensive health and lifestyle assessment to identify their baseline.
3. **Personalised Health Plan:** Based on the assessment, the platform generates a tailored plan featuring 2-3 keystone habits.
4. **Daily Interaction:** User follows their health plan, logging progress via the intuitive dashboard.
5. **Weekly Review:** Platform provides a weekly summary of progress and adjusts the difficulty or introduces new goals as needed.
6. **Subscription Upgrade:** After experiencing the core value in a 14-day trial, user subscribes to unlock premium insights and continuous advanced planning.

## Core Value Proposition
"A personal health platform that actually understands your life. FeedHope doesn't just track what you do; it provides a tailored plan and guides you step-by-step to the healthiest version of yourself."

## MVP Scope (Version 1.0)
- Authentication (Email, Google, Apple)
- Health Assessment Questionnaire
- Personalised Health Plan Generation
- Dashboard (Progress tracking and daily checklist)
- User Profile Management
- Subscription (Stripe integration)

## Future Roadmap (Version 2.0 & Beyond)
- Wearable Device Integration (HealthKit/Google Fit)
- Advanced Health Forecasting and Interventions
- Social Accountability Groups
- B2B Corporate Wellness Dashboard
