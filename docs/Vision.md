# FeedHope – Personal Health Platform: Vision and Mission

## Mission
To build an enterprise-grade personal health platform that empowers individuals to cultivate sustainable, healthier habits through personalized guidance, ongoing support, and actionable insights.

## Vision
To become the globally recognized standard for personal wellness, transforming how millions of users approach their daily health routines and creating a resilient subscription business model that aligns user success with platform growth.

## Long-Term Goals
1. **Global Reach:** Scale to 10+ million active subscribers within 5 years.
2. **Ecosystem Integration:** Seamlessly integrate with major wearable devices (Apple Watch, Fitbit, Garmin, Oura) and health data platforms (Apple HealthKit, Google Fit).
3. **Personalised Guidance:** Develop proprietary health forecasting tools that accurately predict habit formation success and provide preemptive support.
4. **Clinical Validation:** Partner with health institutions to clinically validate the efficacy of FeedHope's interventions in improving physical and mental well-being.
5. **B2B Expansion:** Launch an enterprise wellness solution for corporate clients to improve employee health and productivity.

## Success Metrics
- **User Engagement:** Daily Active Users (DAU) / Monthly Active Users (MAU) ratio > 40%.
- **Retention Rate:** Month-3 subscriber retention rate > 75%.
- **Habit Formation Success:** Percentage of users successfully maintaining a new habit for 60+ days > 50%.
- **Financial Growth:** Achieve $10M Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR) within 24 months of launch.
- **Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) vs Lifetime Value (LTV):** Maintain an LTV:CAC ratio of > 3:1.
